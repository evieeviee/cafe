The Obsidian concept

A dining room designed like a stage.

Every interaction—from arrival and lighting to plating, sound and final coffee—is choreographed as part of the same evening.

Our philosophy
“Luxury is not excess. It is the feeling that every detail belongs exactly where it is.”

The Obsidian Room combines seasonal gastronomy with intimate hospitality. The menu changes in small, deliberate movements, allowing ingredients, textures and aroma to lead the experience rather than decoration.

Spatial Rhythm Lighting, pacing and movement are calibrated to support each course without interrupting the guest experience.
Seasonal Precision Ingredients evolve with harvest, availability and preservation cycles.
Intuitive Hospitality Service is attentive, personal and carefully paced without feeling formal.
Layered Pairings Coffee, tea and non-alcoholic pairings are built around the menu structure.
